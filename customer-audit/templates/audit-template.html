01 — Summary

Bottom line

Warner Super Service is a roughly 40-year-old family-run HVAC & plumbing shop in Oxon Hill, MD (owner Dave Warner; office manager Danielle Stewardson). It has been a Podium customer since December 2021, originally for reviews / messaging and Podium credit-card readers.

The account was effectively re-onboarded in mid-2025 onto Podium Phones (discounted, first 18 months free) plus text & voice AI ("Trudy"). Blended Podium spend today is about $1,428/mo.

It is a financially fragile, operationally thin account: chronic payment arrears, an owner focused on paying down debt, three field techs and a single person in the office. Its two persistent pain points are AI accuracy and a ServiceTitan booking integration that never fully worked.

A June 2026 FSM/CRM upsell ($1,199/mo) was pitched to and liked by Danielle but vetoed by Dave. It was revived and closed on 20 Aug 2026 only because ServiceTitan is ending its Podium integration under a hard deadline — forcing a full migration. Podium sold the entire FSM as the landing spot at the ServiceTitan-refugee rate of $799/mo for year one ($1,199 thereafter) plus a $1,000 one-time onboarding fee. That migration is the current onboarding work (Case 01059453, kickoff 28 Aug 2026).

04 — Sales-call audit

What they were sold on

Each block below is drawn from the full Gong transcript of the call — the specific pitch made, followed by how it has actually played out on the account.

27 Mar 2025 · 40m

Account Review — Text AI pitch

Jon Meyers → Danielle

Pitched

  • Speed-to-lead. Every lead answered in under 60 seconds, including nights and weekends — "you're 80% less likely to convert after 5 minutes."
  • 10× ROI claim. "We sold about $20M of our AI solution and it sold about $200M of HVAC service and sales deals… on average it's like a 10× return." "It can pay for your entire Podium for the year."
  • Autonomous follow-up & upsell. AI re-engages cold leads at 24h and 48h, and cross-sells mid-conversation ("Larry's like your best sales rep").
  • Indistinguishable from a human — the "Josh the detective" anecdote: customer left a 5-star review praising "someone, not an AI."
  • Fast onboarding — 1–2 config calls, ~2 weeks, then light-touch tuning via a Q&A section.
  • Bulk messaging (1,500 credits, already in their plan, unused) — seasonal and "tariff urgency" campaigns suggested.
Since then

They did buy the AI in the 2025 onboarding. No lead-volume check was done on the call despite Danielle flagging "we're not getting a zillion leads." The "can't tell it's AI" selling point later inverted into a liability — per Dave (Aug 2026), a Trudy over-promise produced a bad Google review. Accuracy problems (wrong same-day messaging, wrong service-area denials, false diagnostic-fee claims, name reverting to "Jessica") ran from Dec 2025 through May 2026.

23 Apr 2025 · 37m

Phones + Voice AI pitch

Jon Meyers + Colin Murdock → Danielle

Pitched

  • One number for call + text, with a caller-ID pop showing transcribed call history and "bold-note" summaries.
  • "Press 2 to text instead" deflection to cut call volume; missed-call auto-text and voice AI so "you never miss a call."
  • Revenue anecdote — "Mountaineer Heating & Cooling missed 20% of sales calls… recovered almost $1M that year."
  • Pricing — $150/mo list → 20% Goodman-partner discount → $120/mo for 5 users; first 18 months free on a 2-year term; $877 setup ($377 hardware + $500 network-optimization visit).
  • Contract help — Podium offered to assist with a cash-buyout of their existing BNB VoIP contract, signed only months earlier.
Since then

Phones + voice AI were onboarded in summer 2025. Follow-on friction: a physically stuck "1" key (ticket 00861624), greeting/introscript not playing, the Maryland call-recording announcement playing twice, and the ServiceTitan "green bubble" screen-pop repeatedly breaking. The $500 network-optimization fee plus phone install were the "big lump sum" that triggered the first billing dispute.

3 Jun 2026 · 1h 14m

FSM / CRM demo

Jon Meyers → Danielle

Pitched

  • All-in-one AI operating system — dispatch, calendar, estimates, invoicing, job history and comms in one place; "no more bouncing between ServiceTitan and Podium."
  • Soft booking (AI gathers everything → drag-and-drop onto the calendar) as the low-risk on-ramp; route- and skill-aware scheduling.
  • Membership AI — automated outreach to the ~190 members plus mining the 20,000 non-members; "190 → 1,900 in a year"; "feels like you got a part-time employee back."
  • AI Operator (beta) — plain-English reporting on margins / rep performance / routes, auto follow-up on unaccepted estimates and unpaid invoices, scheduled email digests. Flagged as a possible future paid add-on.
  • Migration — AI-assisted, "not a 6-month nightmare," ~2–3 weeks; techs trained before go-live; price book imported; free Profit Rhino live price book coming.
  • Pricing — $1,199/mo add-on (on top of current ~$1,428); tiered 1–5 techs; possible free months to break the ServiceTitan contract early.
Since then

Danielle was sold ("that's a good price… sounds great"), but Dave killed it on 12 Jun 2026: "he has ZERO desire to change anything right now… paying off outstanding debt, getting stabilized." Jon rebutted (debt = the reason to switch: faster collections, dead-estimate recovery, tap-to-pay at 2.6% vs 3–3.5%), but it stayed dead until ServiceTitan forced the issue.

20 Aug 2026 · 46m

FSM close — with the owner

Kathleen Bowman + Jon Meyers → Dave

Framing: "ServiceTitan has gone completely unresponsive" to Podium's CEO and is ending the integration; hard deadline "the 15th." Podium positioned the FSM as the forced-migration landing spot with priority onboarding.

Pitched to Dave's hot buttons

  • Continuity — keeps the QuickBooks flow (auto integration, Desktop supported, "you don't have to do anything"); data portable back to ServiceTitan later, and "membership agreements are gold" if he sells the business.
  • Soft booking — Dave explicitly wants scheduling control with his small crew and subs.
  • Membership AI as the headline value — the 190 → growth story, bulk campaigns, "3× more likely to sign up if told ahead of time."
  • AI Operator for estimate / invoice / cold-lead follow-up "without hiring anybody."
  • Mobile — full price-book access for owner and tech (a specific ServiceTitan gripe); "we beat ServiceTitan on the mobile experience."
  • Pricing — $1,199/mo, $799/mo for year one (ServiceTitan-refugee rate), $1,000 one-time onboarding, ~2-week priority onboarding, 1–5 tech tier, 12-month term.
Now

This is the deal in onboarding (Case 01059453, kickoff 28 Aug 2026). Salesforce onboarding flags: Phones / AI / Platform onboarding "No"; SOR (system-of-record) onboarding "Yes." Post-migration spend ≈ $2,227/mo in year one, ≈ $2,627/mo year two — before AI Operator.

05 — Friction

Major complaints & recurring friction

AI ("Trudy") reliability recurring · Dec 25 – May 26

Over-promising / confirming appointment times, telling customers same-day needs "24 hours," wrongly denying service areas (Upper Marlboro / 20772), stating a false diagnostic-fee-toward-repair credit, and the AI name reverting to "Jessica" after an upgrade.

Owner attributes at least one bad Google review to a Trudy over-promise (stated on the 20 Aug 2026 call).

ServiceTitan ↔ Podium booking integration never worked Apr – May 26, unresolved

AI-gathered bookings repeatedly failed to land in the ServiceTitan bookings window. Team fully disconnected/reconnected the integration, generated new API keys, re-tested twice — still failing — and escalated to a text-AI engineer. Danielle openly worried "ServiceTitan is potentially cutting out Podium." Still open when the migration was sold.

Unresolved billing disputes Jul 25 → Apr 26

A ~$498–$500 July line item Danielle believes was double-charged, re-raised with Rachel Weiss (Dec 2025) and again with Kathleen (Apr 2026), never closed out. Confusion compounded by CSM handoffs, net-30 terms and a "system transition." The $500 network-optimization + phone install also hit as an unexpected lump.

Chronically behind on payments Feb–Apr 25 · again Apr 26

Arrears on Feb/Mar/Apr 2025 invoices delayed a renewal quote through deal desk; behind again in April 2026. Cause per Danielle: slow shoulder seasons + slow-paying commercial work. Per Dave (2026): actively paying down business debt and "getting stabilized."

Desktop app performance raised Aug 25

Podium desktop app noticeably slowed Danielle's Windows 10 machine; no spell-check or right-click correction. Logged as product feedback.

Phone hardware ticket 00861624 · Nov 25

Desk-phone "1" key physically sticking / auto-repeating. Diagnosed by support as a mis-seated key.

CSM churn & lost context 2025–2026

Kathleen → Rachel Weiss → back to Kathleen. Billing history and AI configuration were re-explained from scratch across each handoff, which is part of why the billing dispute never closed.

Understaffed & fragile structural

The office lost a staff member (passed away, Feb 2026), leaving Danielle alone with an owner who is mostly in the field. Every initiative — especially the membership program that anchors every recent sales pitch — stalls for lack of bandwidth.

07 — Going in

Watch items for the migration

Four things that could bite this onboarding

  1. Spend vs. payment history. Total Podium spend jumps to ≈ $2,227/mo (yr 1) then ≈ $2,627/mo, on an account with repeated arrears and an owner explicitly paying down debt. The year-two step-up from $799 → $1,199 is a churn/dispute risk.
  2. The integration being replaced never worked. The ServiceTitan booking sync was still failing and escalated to engineering in May 2026. The migration now removes that dependency entirely — but under a hard external deadline, so there's little slack if data or price-book import slips.
  3. The membership program is the whole value case — and has never had bandwidth. Every recent pitch (Jun & Aug 2026) leans on Membership AI growing 190 → ~1,900. With one person in the office, adoption support needs to be hands-on or the ROI story collapses.
  4. Unclosed billing dispute + CSM history. The ~$498–$500 item has been open since mid-2025 across two CSM changes. Resolve it before or during kickoff so it doesn't poison the migration relationship.
